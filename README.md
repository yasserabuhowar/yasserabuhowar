### About

I work as a government inspector at the **General Directorate of Consumer Protection, Palestinian Ministry of National Economy**, where my focus is digital legislation, e-commerce regulation, and electronic consumer protection.

My background is in pharmacy (**B.Pharm, October 6 University, 2004**), and I am completing an **MBA in Digital Business Administration** at Al-Quds Open University. I am also a member of the Palestine Standards Institution's technical committee for cosmetics specifications (TC12).

This GitHub is where I turn domain problems from those fields into working prototypes.

### How I work

- **Domain first.** I define the problem, the data sources, and what "correct" means from a pharmacist's and regulator's point of view.
- **AI-assisted engineering.** I build with AI coding tools (mainly Claude) and review, test, and document the output myself.
- **Honest limits.** Every project states what it does *not* do, which assumptions are unvalidated, and where it fails.
